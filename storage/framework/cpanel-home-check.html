About Nesim

Our Story

Back Ground of Organization NEDO is organization established in Ethiopia in 2026 as a Non-Government, non-political, Non-Profit and non-religious Organization Established with the active initiative of Education and development aim to provide knowledge, skills and helping the people learn to think critically to solve their socio-economic problems, make informed decisions through education by helping young generation and children to achieve their dreams and contribute positively to society. NEDO paves the way for quality education and society development with the goal to improve the education status of the rural poor and vulnerable people emphasizing the women and children through concretization, capacity building institution building, bottom up participatory and integrated development framework and acts as a catalyst with its beneficiaries. In addition, this organization is working to change the future for girls and orphan enabling them to fulfill their potential in order to promote confidence, discipline and personal growth. The organization’s aim to paves the way to quality education for those in the remote countryside of Ethiopia to break the low attitude for education and helping them to get skill-based training by integrating education.

Our Approach

How We Do It

A proven, community-driven model that turns intention into lasting impact.

🔍 1

Identify

We partner with local leaders to find the communities with the greatest need and highest motivation for change.

🤝 2

Co-Design

Together with families and teachers, we build a tailored education plan that fits each community's unique context.

🏫 3

Build & Train

We construct classrooms, supply materials, and train local educators to deliver quality instruction.

📈 4

Measure & Grow

Rigorous tracking ensures every student progresses — and every lesson learned scales to the next village.

A Message from Our Chairman
“When I founded Nesim, I carried a simple conviction: that the potential of every Ethiopian child is boundless — but opportunity is not equally distributed. Today, as I visit the communities we serve and see young people who once struggled to find a book now mentoring the next generation, I know our work matters. We are not just building schools or drilling wells; we are building a movement of people who believe in each other. I invite you to join us — as a volunteer, a donor, or simply someone who shares this vision for a brighter Ethiopia.”

Dr Ali Mohammed
